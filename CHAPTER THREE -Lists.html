Scrambled Eggs

Eggs are one of my favourite foods, Here is a recipe for deliciously rich scrambled eggs.

Ingredients

    2 eggs 1tbs butter 2tbs cream

    Method

      Melt butter in a frying pan over a medium heat Gently mix the eggs and cream in a bowl Once butter has melted add cream and eggs Using a spatula fold the eggs from the edge of the pa to the center every 20 seconds (as if you are making a omelette) When the eggs are still moist remove from the heat (it will continue to cook on the plate until served)
